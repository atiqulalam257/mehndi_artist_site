Mere Baare Mein

Har design mein dil ki ek baat

Main Babli, ek mahila mehndi artist hoon. Mehndi mere liye sirf design nahi, ek bhavna hai — jo dulhan ke haath par sajti hai aur ghar ki yaadon mein rehti hai.

Har booking ko main khud dekhti hoon: pehle aapki pasand samajhti hoon, phir aapke rang, dress aur mauke ke hisaab se design banati hoon, taaki aap jo chahein wahi dikhe.

  • Naazuk aur saaf lines, shuddh organic mehndi
  • Ghar par aakar kaam — aapka waqt aur aaram bachta hai
  • Bridal se lekar minimal designs tak, har style mein anubhav
